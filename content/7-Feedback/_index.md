### General Assessment

**1. Work Environment**
The work environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always ready to assist whenever I face difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, which helps me focus better. However, I think adding a few social gatherings or team-building activities would help everyone get to know each other better.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
My mentor provides detailed guidance, clearly explains concepts I don't understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The team admin assists with procedures and documentation, creating favorable conditions for my work. I really appreciate that my mentor allows me to experiment and solve problems myself rather than just handing me the answers.

**3. Alignment Between Work and Field of Study**
The tasks assigned to me align with the knowledge I gained at school while also introducing me to new areas I hadn't encountered before. As a result, I have been able to both reinforce my foundational knowledge and acquire practical skills.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**
During my internship, I have learned many new skills, such as using project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication within a corporate setting. My mentor has also shared valuable practical experiences that help me better shape my career path.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects one another, maintaining a serious yet cheerful work atmosphere. When facing urgent projects, everyone pulls together and supports each other, regardless of their position. This makes me feel like a true part of the team, even though I am just an intern. **6. Intern policies/benefits**
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible scheduling when needed. Additionally, the opportunity to participate in internal training sessions is a major plus.
