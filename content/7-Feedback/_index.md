### General Assessment

**1. Work Environment**
The work environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always ready to assist whenever I face difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, which helps me focus better. However, I think adding a few social gatherings or team-building activities would help everyone get to know each other better.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admin**
My mentor provides detailed guidance, offers clear explanations when I don't understand something, and always encourages me to ask questions. The team admin assists with procedures and documentation, creating favorable conditions for my work. I really appreciate that my mentor allows me to experiment and solve problems myself rather than simply providing the answers.

**3. Alignment Between Work and Field of Study**
The tasks assigned to me align well with the knowledge I gained at university while also introducing me to new areas I hadn't encountered before. As a result, I have been able to both reinforce my foundational knowledge and acquire practical skills.

**4. **Opportunities for learning & skill development**
During my internship, I acquired various new skills, such as using project management tools, teamwork, and professional corporate communication. My mentor also shared valuable practical insights that helped me better shape my career path.

**5. Culture & team spirit**
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects one another, maintaining a serious work ethic while keeping the atmosphere enjoyable. When facing urgent projects, the team pulls together and offers support regardless of job titles. This made me feel like a true part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Intern policies & benefits**
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible scheduling when needed. Additionally, the opportunity to participate in internal training sessions was a major plus.
